Questions? We Have Answers.

Frequently asked questions.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?+
Candidates include those with missing teeth, failing teeth, or ill‑fitting dentures. Bone health matters, but even patients with bone loss can often qualify through techniques like All‑on‑4 or bone grafting.
Can I get fixed teeth in one day?+
Some qualifying cases may receive fixed teeth the same day, while final zirconia timing can vary by treatment plan. 3D imaging and digital design plan the prosthetic before surgery, streamlining the overall timeline.
How much do dental implants cost?+
Costs vary by case, but single‑arch treatment starts around $19,500 and double‑arch around $37,500, with financing available from $289 and $489 per month respectively for qualifying patients.
What is All‑on‑4?+
All‑on‑4 uses four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth, often avoiding the need for bone grafting and reducing both cost and recovery time.
Will dental implants hurt?+
Surgery is performed under local anesthesia, sedation, or general anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during the procedure. Post‑operative discomfort is typically mild and manageable with medication.
How long do dental implants last?+
The implant post can last indefinitely with proper care, while the prosthetic crown may need replacement after 10–15 years of normal wear.
What is zirconia?+
Zirconia is a metal‑free, non‑porous ceramic material that resists staining and closely mimics the appearance of natural teeth, fully customized to your facial structure.
What does recovery look like?+
Recovery typically includes mild swelling, discomfort or bruising for a few days, with most patients returning to normal activities quickly. Full osseointegration takes 3–6 months.
